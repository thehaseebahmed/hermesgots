# A bot's settings are multi-step and split by ownership

A bot's settings grew as six tabs on one screen and a settings owner nobody could name, so Settings > Bot became its own multi-step screen: a grouped list of pages — Gots Specific Settings over Hermes Settings — that is sub-nav beside the page at 1024px and wider and a pushed stack below it, and every setting is grouped by who owns its value: the Gots plugin's own host values and the app's own are **Gots settings**, and a knob on the bot's Hermes is a **Hermes setting**, named as the Hermes Dashboard names it. Considered: keeping the tab strip (it ran out of width at 390px and gave subtitles and group headers nowhere to live) and grouping by subject instead of ownership (blurry — the triage model is a Gots concept stored on the host); ownership is the line the glossary states in one sentence and every page applies the same way, which is what let *API key* become *Server key* and *Fallback models* become *Fallback providers* on the grounds that Hermes names its own knobs.
