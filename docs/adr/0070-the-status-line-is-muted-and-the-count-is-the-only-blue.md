# The status line is muted, the thought row says its own word, and the count is the only blue

**Amends [ADR 0069](0069-one-working-look-and-one-chat-face.md) and
[ADR 0066](0066-the-status-line-wears-a-rotating-word-and-a-loading-thread-says-so.md)**: 0066's
accent on the line and its loader, its loaderless wait, and its count as a work row of its own; 0069's
thinking row wearing the line's word in the line's look. What those two decided elsewhere — 0069's
code face, its one loader for a thread whose first read is out, and the row shape it inherits from
ADR 0050 — stands.

**A working reply said one thing twice, in the colour meant for what you act on.** A thought row
still arriving and the status line beneath it drew the same fact in the same accent — and neither
holds anything to press. The one pressable thing in that cluster, the count of subagents, which
opens their sheet, was the quieter of the two: info blue beside an accent loader. Before its first
reasoning word the turn also drew a spinner over no words at all, placed behind its last call, so
the row meaning "thinking" sat *below* the tool row it was about.

**The rows a working reply ends on are three, in one order: thought, tools, status.** The order
governs the live cluster at the reply's foot — a thought row above the tool row whose call it led
into, one per cluster, and one status line under the reply's last words — and it moves nothing
already drawn: a thought row stays where its words streamed, above the call it led into.

**A turn with no word of thinking draws no row.** The status line says the turn is thinking until
one arrives, so a working turn says it in exactly one place, never two. While its words arrive the
thought row turns a loader beside the literal word "thinking", both in the row's own muted colour;
once measured it reads "Thought for 12s". The rotating words belong to the status line alone.

**The status line and its loader are muted.** The line says nothing while a thought row above says
thinking, and nothing while a call runs save the count of subagents at work. When the turn waits on
the reader, the wait words take the place of the busy words with the loader still turning beside
them. Once the turn is done and its subagents still run, the count is the line's only words. The
count is the only thing in the cluster in the info colour, and a loader wears that colour only when
it turns for the count alone; in every other case it wears the colour of the words it turns for. The
line turns one loader, and the count as a suffix never adds a second. A failed or stopped reply draws
no status line — the error or "Stopped" stands in its place.

**What was rejected.** Keeping the accent on the line: with the thought row muted, the accent's only
job left in the cluster would be to say "working", while the one control a reader can press stayed
blue and quiet. Colour now answers "can I press this" and motion answers "is something happening".
Keeping the wordless row: beyond breaking the order, it named the same moment as the line's words,
and two accounts of one moment read as two events.

**What stays literal.** A finished row's "Thought for 12s", a tool row's "Running bash…" and
"Ran 2 commands", a failed call's reason under its row, and the wait words stay as written; only the
thinking word moved, from the line into the row. A thread whose first read is still out says
"Catching up…" in one rotating word where the reply would work.
