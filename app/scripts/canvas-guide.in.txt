# Canvas

A canvas is one HTML page the user keeps and asks you to change. Write it whole with `canvas_write`;
read it with `canvas_read` first.

## The user's design

Their aesthetic wins: a named look, a brand, a palette, a font or a reference is the design and the
job; what follows is a floor, filling only what the user leaves unsaid.
## The floor

One surface, one job, a real hierarchy: a title, a next step, one deliberate accent, and its empty,
loading and error states, each saying what to do, with no explanatory copy.
## Colour and classes

The app draws the page, its theme on `:root` as CSS variables:

`--background --foreground --card --card-foreground --primary --primary-foreground --secondary
--secondary-foreground --muted --muted-foreground --accent --accent-foreground --border
--destructive --success --warning --info --radius`

Use them and it follows the mode; a hardcoded colour breaks in dark, so spend colour on the tokens,
`--primary` for what the page is for. Tailwind v4 compiles classes from the same tokens, so
`bg-primary`, `text-foreground`, `border-border`, `rounded-lg`, `p-4`, `md:` and `dark:` work, every
token having `bg-`, `text-` and `border-`. Only markup classes compile; `<style>` is for the rest.
## Phone first

Most open on a phone: lay it out for 390px first, then breathe from 768px; nothing scrolls sideways.
Tappable is 44px tall and never behind hover; typed-into type is 16px. Keep the foot clear of the
phone's bar: `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`.

## Points

A message may end with `[POINT — Total | selector: #total]`: the reader tapped that element while
asking; the label says what they tapped, the selector finds it.

## Design skills

Three load with `skill_view`: `gots:impeccable` when the page should look like something,
`gots:web-design-guidelines` when it has to pass a review, `gots:transitions-dev` when it moves (CSS
to paste, not to import). `plugin/canvas/DESIGN.md` is the design system, and the page gets the tokens
above and no others.
## The sandbox

- A form's submit is cancelled in place: nothing navigates, so handle the click.
- An `http(s)` link opens in the app on tap: use one when the reader should leave.
- Remote images over https are allowed; nothing else loads from outside:
  a `<script src>`, CDN `<link>`, `http://` image, `<iframe>`, `<object>`, `<embed>`, `<base>`, meta
  refresh or `url(https://…)` refuses the write whole. No `RTCPeerConnection`.

## The document

One complete file: `<!doctype html>`, `<html lang="en">`, a `<head>` with a `<title>` and viewport
meta, styles in `<style>`, script in `<script>`. Under 256 KB. No secrets: it is readable by all.

## Its data

A canvas keeps documents of `{collection, id, doc, at}`. The page has `canvas.data.list` and
`canvas.data.get`, and `canvas.data.put` and `canvas.data.remove`, each taking a collection and, but
for `list`, an id; you have `canvas_data_read` and `canvas_data_write`. Any of the bot's sessions
except another canvas's reaches it by title or id, which `canvas_list` names; `canvas` here is this
canvas. 1 MB a canvas.
## The host, from the page

It reads four host shapes — `canvas.read("usage", {provider})`, and `canvas.read("routines")`,
`canvas.read("projects")` or `canvas.read("sessions")` — and may call a tool its version declares,
named in `tools` on `canvas_write` or `canvas_edit`: `canvas.tool.call("<server>.<tool>", args)`, which
asks once per tool on its first call. `canvas_edit` takes find-and-replace pairs, each `find` matching
once, all of them or none.
